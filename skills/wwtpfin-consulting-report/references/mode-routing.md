# Mode routing

Select report modules from the actual transaction responsibilities, not from the acronym alone. The shared analytical core always remains available; the items below are additions or substitutions.

## Observed modes

### TOT: transfer, operation and return

Use when an existing facility or operating right is transferred for operation without a new-build obligation.

- Add: existing-asset scope and condition, ownership and debt, valuation and transfer consideration, historical operation, transition and handover, transfer-price funding, operating performance and return conditions.
- Financial emphasis: transfer consideration, existing-asset amortization or depreciation basis, historical volume and cost, operating cash flow, renewal responsibility and terminal return.
- Do not add construction financing, construction-period performance or engineering-scheme chapters unless the input separately establishes a rehabilitation or expansion obligation.
- Typical user-required evidence: valuation report, title and debt confirmation, historical operating records, transfer approval, asset list and condition survey.

### BOT: build, operate and transfer

Use when the concessionaire finances or undertakes new construction or expansion before operation.

- Add: necessity and capacity basis, construction scope, investment and schedule, construction financing, ramp-up, construction and operation performance, completion acceptance and terminal transfer.
- Financial emphasis: construction drawdown and interest, total investment, capital structure, operating ramp-up, debt service, renewal investment and transfer condition.
- Keep engineering detail proportional: report the design boundary and decisive scale assumptions; route structure lists and process calculations to supplements or appendices.
- Typical user-required evidence: approved feasibility/design documents, investment approval, land and environmental approvals, construction schedule, financing terms and acceptance requirements.

### Government construction plus BOT operation

Use when construction and later concession operation are explicitly split between government and operator.

- Separate the construction owner, financing source, completion responsibility and operating-right grant; never collapse them into ordinary BOT.
- State which construction costs enter the operator's cash flow and which remain government-side; do not invent project-company construction financing.
- Add interface conditions for handover from builder to operator, defect responsibility, operating commencement and tariff or service-fee start.

## Unobserved modes: conservative semantic projection

The five benchmark reports do not validate the following variants. Use these modules only when project evidence establishes the corresponding responsibilities.

- **ROT:** combine the TOT existing-asset baseline with a distinct rehabilitation scope, rehabilitation investment, performance-restoration target, shutdown/interface risk and post-rehabilitation acceptance.
- **BOOT:** add concession-period ownership, asset recognition and financing consequences, tax treatment and the terminal ownership transfer; do not assume ownership merely from the acronym if the agreement says otherwise.
- **DBFOT:** add design responsibility, design-change control and performance guarantees to the BOT modules; keep design facts user-supplied unless verified source material supports them.
- **O&M:** omit acquisition and construction investment unless explicitly present; emphasize service boundary, fee mechanism, historical operating baseline, KPI and deductions, asset custody, renewal responsibility and exit handover.

## Conflict rule

When the acronym conflicts with project facts, report the conflict and route by verified responsibilities. Do not silently choose the more familiar mode.
