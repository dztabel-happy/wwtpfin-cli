# Benchmark-derived consulting methods

This reference records the method extracted from five original consulting reports. It is a method reference, not a template library: do not copy their prose, project names, values or local chapter numbering.

Project identities and source fingerprints remain in the private benchmark governance archive. This distributed reference contains reusable methods only.

## Stable method across cases

1. Start with the decision, decisive metrics, evidence limits and immediate conditions; background exists to explain the decision, not to delay it.
2. Keep a common chain: project boundary → transaction responsibilities → investment/funding → volume/tariff/cost/tax → financial result → sensitivity/decision boundary → risk/compliance/performance/exit → conditions and evidence requests.
3. Build analytical passages as `conclusion → basis → decisive data → risk or limitation → action`. A conclusion without basis is unsupported; data without a conclusion is a dump.
4. Separate three things that source reports often mix: verified project facts, shared rules, and user-required material. Never promote one into another.
5. Summarize long schedules in the body. Keep the complete calculation package in the verified run; select only client-useful schedules for Word.

## Stable financial core across cases

All five original reports contain a stable `项目盈利能力分析` function even though their chapter numbers and transaction modes differ. Treat that function, not the full Word page count, as the baseline for the CLI financial core. It consistently covers:

- calculation boundary, period, tax basis and decisive assumptions;
- investment or transfer consideration and funding structure;
- volume, tariff, revenue, operating cost and tax;
- profitability, cash flow, debt service and payback;
- sensitivity or decision-boundary analysis;
- a criterion-backed financial conclusion, material limitations and immediate actions.

TOT reports emphasize transfer consideration, historical operation and operating return. BOT reports add construction investment, drawdown, interest, ramp-up and terminal transfer. These are mode-specific inputs to one financial reasoning chain, not separate prose templates. Evaluate the CLI core on topic coverage, numeric fidelity, traceability, causal interpretation, duplicate suppression and gap isolation. Evaluate the complete Word report separately on source-backed non-financial modules.

## Structural baseline from the five originals

The original Word deliverables are full concession consulting reports, not financial briefs. Their Word metadata and document structures span:

| Measure | Observed range |
| --- | ---: |
| Pages recorded in Word metadata | 124–173 |
| Narrative characters outside tables | 42,334–57,158 |
| Heading paragraphs | 176–207 |
| Tables | 22–39 |
| Table rows | 315–586 |

These are diagnostic ranges, not page or word quotas. Scanned approvals and long schedules can inflate them. They do establish one hard lesson: a 20-page financial brief containing only a small fraction of the available narrative and client-facing schedules is not equivalent to the benchmark deliverable. When the generated report is materially narrower, the plan must show whether the cause is missing user material, an inapplicable module or incorrect routing.

Across the originals, the depth comes from subtopics, not filler: necessity, project and engineering boundary, mode feasibility, charging and transaction structure, investment and financing, financial analysis, comparison and participation, compliance, risk, performance, supervision, guarantees, adjustment, termination, handover, conclusions and appendices. Preserve that analytical breadth without copying local prose.

## Reader-facing table method

### Usually suitable for the body

- Project scope and transaction summary.
- Adopted total investment and funding structure.
- Historical or forecast volume and ramp-up summary when it drives revenue.
- Tariff, revenue, operating-cost and tax-basis summaries.
- Key profitability, solvency and break-even indicators.
- Sensitivity results that expose a decision boundary.
- Concise risk allocation or performance summary when it supports a recommendation.

### Usually appendix material

- Full annual cash-flow, profit, debt-service and tax schedules.
- Detailed investment line items, structure lists and engineering quantities.
- Long monthly operating, water-quality or water-volume histories.
- Full performance scoring and deduction matrices.
- Full risk registers and early-termination compensation schedules.

Appendix candidates become client content only when the reader needs them to understand, use or review a conclusion. Duplicate wide/long variants, exhaustive ledgers and intermediate schedules remain workpapers in the verified run; this is not loss of calculation coverage.

### Audit only

- Parameter inventories, formula registers, lineage tables, reconciliation residuals, internal checks, implementation switches and exhaustive scenario traces.

### Technical workpapers

- Duplicate presentations of the same financial schedule.
- Exhaustive annual ledgers not cited by a conclusion.
- Machine-oriented totals, intermediate schedules and diagnostic expansions.

Placement is purpose-driven. A workpaper may move into an appendix when a specific client decision depends on it and the plan states why; audit-only material never becomes client content.

## Content normally requiring user material

- Engineering design, process selection, site layout and construction quantities.
- Asset valuation originals, ownership, debt, encumbrance and condition evidence.
- Land, planning, environmental, energy, approval and acceptance originals.
- Historical operation, water quality, volume, maintenance and incident records.
- Local demand forecasts, socioeconomic context and project-specific policy facts.
- Organization-specific governance, performance, risk, guarantee, handover and termination terms.
- Maps, process diagrams, approval scans and user-mandated conclusions outside the financial model.

If such material changes a calculation, evidence state or decision, return it to the input and create a new verified run. Otherwise register it as a sourced supplement or `user_required` item.

## Evidence boundary

The benchmark set directly supports TOT, BOT and separated government-construction-plus-BOT-operation patterns. It does not directly support ROT, BOOT, DBFOT or pure O&M. Guidance for those modes in `mode-routing.md` is a conservative responsibility-based projection, not benchmark proof.
